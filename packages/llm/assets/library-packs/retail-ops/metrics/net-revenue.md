# Definition

Net revenue = gross order value − refunds − discounts − taxes collected.

- Count an order when it is **completed**, not when it is placed.
- Refunds subtract in the period they are issued, not the period of the
  original sale.
- Gift-card purchases count when the card is redeemed, not when sold.

# Why it matters

Teams that mix gross and net revenue in different reports disagree by
5–15% and burn meetings reconciling. Pick this definition (or edit it),
approve it, and every assistant answer will use the same number.
